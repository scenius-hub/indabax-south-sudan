About Us

IndabaX South Sudan 2026 is Scenius Hub's flagship AI community and industry programme, building on the momentum of IndabaX South Sudan 2025 — the country's first AI summit. Rather than a single conference, IndabaX South sudan 2026 is a multi-stage journey that brings together students, developers, researchers, industry professionals, and innovators to learn, connect, and build practical AI solutions for South Sudan.

The programme follows a Learn → Engage → Build → Showcase model delivered across three connected stages: two hands-on practical meetups, and a culminating Final Indaba Event featuring a keynote, industry engagement, and a hackathon showcase.

This structured pathway is designed to move participants from foundational machine learning skills, to building their own AI systems, to applying those skills against real, locally relevant industry challenges — creating a stronger, more connected AI ecosystem in South Sudan.

The Programme Journey

  • Meetup 1 — Introduction to Machine Learning: A fully hands-on 3-hour workshop where participants learn core ML concepts and train and evaluate their own machine learning model.
  • Meetup 2 — Building a Small Language Model: A fully hands-on 3-hour workshop where participants build on Meetup 1 to construct, train, and prompt their own small language model.
  • Final AI Indaba Event: A keynote address, industry engagement sessions, and the culmination of the AI Hackathon — where teams present locally relevant AI solutions to a panel of judges from industry, academia, and government.

What to Expect

  • Hands-On Workshops: Build real, working machine learning models and a small language model through fully practical, guided sessions — not just theory.
  • Keynote Presentations: Hear from leading African and international experts on AI, data science, and emerging technologies.
  • Industry Engagement: Learn directly from companies applying AI in telecommunications, financial services, fintech, banking, and beyond.
  • Hackathon: Develop creative solutions using real, locally relevant datasets and industry-defined challenges.
  • Exhibitions: Organizations and startups will showcase AI-powered products, tools, and services driving change in our communities.
  • Networking: Connect with peers, mentors, and potential collaborators who share a vision of using technology for sustainable development.

Why Attend?

  • Build practical, hands-on machine learning and AI skills — leave with models you built yourself.
  • Learn from experts and practitioners in the AI field.
  • Gain visibility for your projects and connect directly with industry.
  • Collaborate on innovative ideas that address real-world South Sudanese challenges.
  • Be part of the movement putting South Sudan on the map of AI development in Africa.
