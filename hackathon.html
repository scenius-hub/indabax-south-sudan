Hackathon @ IndabaX South Sudan 2026

Join our AI Hackathon to collaborate, innovate, and compete in solving real-world South Sudanese challenges using artificial intelligence for social good.

Overview

IndabaX South Sudan 2026 invites developers, data scientists, students, and AI enthusiasts to take part in an AI Hackathon designed to drive local, real-world impact. The hackathon is the culmination of the two hands-on Indaba meetups, giving participants the chance to apply their machine learning and language model skills to a live industry challenge.

Challenge & Format

Full details on the hackathon challenge, dataset, and platform will be announced closer to the event, developed together with our industry partners. Check back here or follow Scenius Hub's channels for updates.

Hackathon Guidelines & Eligibility Criteria

Who Can Participate

  • Open to All: Any Student interested in AI, machine learning, and data science are welcome to participate.
  • Team Size: Teams must have 1–3 members.
  • Skill Levels: Participants of all experience levels are encouraged to join. We value both beginners and seasoned developers.
  • Participation Mode: Details to be announced.

Additional Notes

  • Mentors will be available to guide teams throughout the hackathon.
  • Workspace and support will be available at Scenius Hub.
  • An info session will be announced closer to the event.

Prizes

Top teams will win exciting prizes and swag, as well as prizes from our partners.
