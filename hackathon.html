Hackathon @ IndabaX South Sudan 2026

Join our AI Hackathon to collaborate, innovate, and compete in solving real-world South Sudanese challenges using artificial intelligence for social good.

Overview

IndabaX South Sudan 2026 invites developers, data scientists, students, and AI enthusiasts to take part in an AI Hackathon designed to drive local, real-world impact. The hackathon is the culmination of the two hands-on Indaba meetups, giving participants the chance to apply their machine learning skills to a live challenge grounded in real South Sudanese data.

Challenge & Format

📅 Launches Thursday, 24th September 2026 at 10:00 PM · Runs for 6 days · Ends 30th September 2026

This year's challenge asks participants to build a model that predicts food insecurity risk across South Sudan's counties, using real historical data on food security classifications and agricultural production sourced from South Sudan's National Bureau of Statistics via CLIMIS.

The hackathon is hosted on Zindi. Full challenge details, the dataset, rules, and evaluation criteria are available on the competition page:

Hackathon Guidelines & Eligibility Criteria

Who Can Participate

  • Open to All: Any person interested in AI, machine learning, and data science is welcome to participate.
  • Team Size: Teams must have 1–3 members.
  • Skill Levels: Participants of all experience levels are encouraged to join. We value both beginners and seasoned developers.

Additional Notes

  • Access to the competition requires a registration code, sent to participants after signing up.
  • Workspace and support will be available at Scenius Hub.
  • Questions can be raised in the discussion/chat tab on the Zindi challenge page.
